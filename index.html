Constraints
Paying generators to change their output because the grid can't carry power to where it's needed – typically paying Scottish wind farms to turn down and gas plants nearer demand to turn up. Now the bulk of the cost.
Reserve
Keeping power stations, batteries and other sources on standby, ready to raise or cut output at short notice if demand or supply changes unexpectedly, such as a large power station tripping off.
Other
Smaller items: matching overall supply and demand second to second ("energy imbalance"), keeping the grid's frequency close to 50 Hz, and services such as restarting the grid after a blackout. Can net negative, as NESO sometimes sells energy back.
